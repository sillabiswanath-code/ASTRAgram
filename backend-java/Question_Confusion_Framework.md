# Question Confusion Framework

## Converting Simple Statements into Thinking Questions

### Objective

Transform direct statements into questions that require students to:

* Think before answering
* Apply understanding
* Avoid guessing from keywords
* Demonstrate conceptual knowledge
* Detect misconceptions

The question should remain simple in language but indirect in structure.

---

# Rule 1: Never Ask What Was Directly Said

## Weak

Force is a push or pull.

Question:

What is force?

---

## Better

Which situation would NOT require a force?

---

## Better Again

A person changes the motion of an object without touching it.

What might be happening?

---

# Rule 2: Convert Definitions into Situations

## Original Statement

Plants need sunlight for photosynthesis.

---

## Weak Question

Why do plants need sunlight?

---

## Better Question

A plant is kept in a dark room for several weeks.

What is most likely to happen?

---

# Rule 3: Replace Facts with Observations

## Original Statement

Magnets attract iron.

---

## Weak Question

What do magnets attract?

---

## Better Question

A child places several objects near a magnet.

Which object is most likely to move toward it?

---

# Rule 4: Ask About Effects Instead of Causes

## Original Statement

Water boils at 100°C.

---

## Weak Question

At what temperature does water boil?

---

## Better Question

Bubbles begin forming rapidly throughout a container of water.

What is most likely happening?

---

# Rule 5: Hide the Concept Name

## Original Statement

Evaporation changes liquid into gas.

---

## Weak Question

What is evaporation?

---

## Better Question

A puddle disappears after a sunny day.

Which process best explains this?

---

# Rule 6: Use Everyday Examples

## Original Statement

Friction opposes motion.

---

## Weak Question

What is friction?

---

## Better Question

Why is it harder to slide a heavy box across a rough floor than a smooth floor?

---

# Rule 7: Ask Students to Identify Mistakes

## Original Statement

The Earth revolves around the Sun.

---

## Better Question

A student says:

"The Sun revolves around the Earth every year."

What is incorrect about this statement?

---

# Rule 8: Reverse the Logic

## Original Statement

Plants need water to grow.

---

## Direct Question

Why do plants need water?

---

## Reverse Question

A plant receives sunlight but no water.

Which outcome is most likely?

---

# Rule 9: Ask What Cannot Happen

Students usually recognize correct answers easily.

They struggle more with exceptions.

---

## Original Statement

Force can change motion.

---

## Better Question

Which of the following changes is least likely to occur without a force?

---

# Rule 10: Ask for Best Explanation

## Original Statement

Objects fall due to gravity.

---

## Better Question

Which explanation best describes why a dropped ball moves toward the ground?

---

# Rule 11: Introduce a Character

Students engage more with people than abstract facts.

---

## Original Statement

Push moves objects away.

---

## Better Question

Ravi places his hand on a cart and moves it forward.

Which type of force is he applying?

---

# Rule 12: Ask About Comparison

## Original Statement

Push and pull are types of force.

---

## Better Question

How is pushing a door different from pulling a drawer?

---

# Rule 13: Use Incomplete Information

## Original Statement

Birds use wings to fly.

---

## Better Question

An animal moves through the air without jumping.

Which feature is most likely helping it?

---

# Rule 14: Test Understanding Through Classification

## Original Statement

Pushing and kicking are forces.

---

## Better Question

Which activity belongs in the same category as kicking a football?

---

# Rule 15: Use "Most Likely"

This prevents obvious answers.

---

## Original Statement

Roots absorb water.

---

## Better Question

Which part of a plant is most likely responsible for taking in water from the soil?

---

# Rule 16: Use "Best Describes"

Makes students compare options.

---

## Original Statement

Photosynthesis makes food for plants.

---

## Better Question

Which statement best describes the purpose of photosynthesis?

---

# Rule 17: Create a Mini Story

## Original Statement

Force changes direction.

---

## Better Question

A moving football suddenly turns after hitting a player's foot.

What most likely caused the change?

---

# Rule 18: Introduce a Common Misconception

## Original Statement

Heavier objects are not always faster.

---

## Better Question

A student claims:

"Heavier objects always fall faster."

Which response is most accurate?

---

# Rule 19: Ask for Evidence

## Original Statement

Air occupies space.

---

## Better Question

Which observation provides the strongest evidence that air occupies space?

---

# Rule 20: Make the Student Predict

## Original Statement

Seeds grow into plants.

---

## Better Question

A seed is planted in moist soil and receives sunlight.

What is most likely to happen next?

# Universal Conversion Prompt

Given a topic and a statement:

1. Do NOT ask the statement directly.
2. Hide the concept name whenever possible.
3. Convert definitions into situations.
4. Convert facts into observations.
5. Prefer real-life examples.
6. Use indirect questioning.
7. Introduce misconceptions when appropriate.
8. Test understanding instead of memorization.
9. Keep language simple.
10. Make the student think before recognizing the answer.

Generate:

* Easy Version
* Medium Version
* Hard Version

for every concept provided.
